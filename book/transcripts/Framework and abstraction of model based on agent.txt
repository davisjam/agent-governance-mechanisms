# Framework and abstraction of model based on agent

Well, one of course might ask, if this is a framework, how does it change as we modify variables in the framework?
 And one of the chief variables of interest is the caliber of the agent.
 In other words, its reasoning element and its ability to develop and reason over increasingly complex systems.
 And to this, my reply is, one of the advantages of a model-based approach is that models are layers of abstractions.
 And the stronger the model, the more abstraction it can be trusted to reify without direct supervision.
 So as the models become more capable, the thing that will change is the scope of system for which an individual engineer is responsible.
 And the levels of abstraction at which the engineer is reasoning.
 But the approach itself will not change.
 The main thing is the state shift from the engineer being responsible for the implementation to the engineer being responsible for the design.
 Once that shift happens, the only difference will be of scale, not of kind.
 This text is primarily preaching that the first and most important change is of kind from managing the implementation to managing the design.
