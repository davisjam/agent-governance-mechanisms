Before any tool or model comes a shift in how you see the work. A coding agent builds
exactly what its instructions describe, the way a printer does, so when the build is wrong
the first place to look is the instructions, not the machine. This Part sets that posture.
You will meet the whole method through one real system built by a fleet of agents, see the
loop every agent runs and the few levers that steer it, and follow the short derivation from
what these models can and cannot do to why the method takes the shape it does. It ends by
naming the seat you now sit in: the engineer's, not the coder's — the same lifecycle as ever,
a different job.
