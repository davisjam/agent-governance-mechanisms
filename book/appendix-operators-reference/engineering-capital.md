*Is the environment becoming more valuable, or are we just moving faster?*

Not a single metric — an operator's assessment across five indicators. Green means recurring failures are
disappearing into reused mechanisms while per-change human attention falls. Red means the apparatus is growing
past its fit, or holding capital that outlived it.

This is the deck's endpoint. The book argues that the unit of progress is not code but the conversion of
recurring judgment into durable infrastructure. Engineering capital is that argument made operational — the
accumulated governance that lowers the reasoning a future change demands. It is an assessment, not a gauge.

### The five indicators

1. **Support ratio.** Support-apparatus lines over production lines — a structural stock, checked quarterly
   or when comparing projects. It settles around 3x at maturity. One indicator here, not the headline.
2. **Mechanism reuse.** Are mechanisms reused across sites rather than re-invented? Soft-gap — no declared
   reuse metric; the proxy is the catalogue's per-entry known-uses count.
3. **Recurring failures.** Are the same failure classes disappearing? Soft-gap — a failure-class construct,
   no declared count.
4. **Manual judgment.** Is per-change human attention falling as classes convert? Reads oversight amortization.
5. **Drift stability.** Do the sync gates hold steady green over time? Reads Model-sync efficacy.

Two declared numbers anchor the assessment: support ratio (around 3x at maturity) and control growth (project
lints 0 to 747, gate scripts 0 to 102). The other three render as assessment signals.

### Reading the banner

- **Green — capital growing.** Repeated failures disappearing; mechanisms reused, not re-invented; reviews
  shrinking per change; drift gates steady green.
- **Yellow — bureaucracy risk.** Duplicate mechanisms appearing; stale constraints; friction rising without
  new capability; support ratio climbing past its fit with no capability payoff.
- **Red — capital decaying.** Retired mechanisms never removed; the apparatus pushed past its point of fit;
  capital that outlived its fit and is no longer subtractively reconciled.

### What this projects

The compounding-governance proposition: as recurring judgment converts into durable mechanisms, an increasing
fraction of future effort extends capability rather than re-solving understood problems — for as long as the
capital stays fit. Plus the *compounding* concept (the engineering-capital stock), the governance-adaptation
to capability-amplification composition, and the proposition's own caveat — returns attach to capital
quality, not apparatus quantity. The Red band is that bounded claim rendered.
