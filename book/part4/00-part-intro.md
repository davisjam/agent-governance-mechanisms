Most codebases you meet are not clean fields; they are brownfield, and an agent left loose
makes them worse. This Part turns the method into practice. You will start from the drifted
wiki you already have and migrate it in stages into a model an agent can trust, meet the three
skills that let a fleet self-operate, self-govern, and explain itself, and learn to size each
transformation to the leap you can trust it to make. Then the honest part: what three months
of building this way actually taught — where agents amplify your reach, where they manufacture
churn, and why "done" is a claim you have to verify rather than a fact. It closes on generative
validation, the move that hunts for the counterexample your example tests never thought to ask
about.
