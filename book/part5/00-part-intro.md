Here the book grounds every claim in the one system it was built from. The setting is document
accessibility: a legal deadline, a crushing manual cost, and files far more tangled than they
look. You will follow the project from a committee meeting through the stages of its build —
the velocity curve, the apparatus that outgrew the product, the deploy surprises the tests
could not see — and then look inside the system that resulted, the same design read three
ways. The Part ends by climbing the ladder the whole method traces: from reviewing every line
yourself, to dispatching a crew, to zoning the code, to modeling it, the road that turned one
engineer into the operator of a fleet.
